Pancake Recipe

Description: A Pancake is a flat, round cake made from a batter of flour, eggs, and milk, cooked on a hot surface like a griddle or pan.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Egg
  • Vanilla Extract

Steps

  1. Measure the flour lightly and add the other dry ingredients well.
  2. Use a fork to wisk the egg before adding any wet ingredients to the mix.
  3. Make a well in the middle and add the rest of the ingredients.
  4. Mix the flour from the outside in.
  5. Heat your pan or griddle first over medium-high heat.
  6. Once hot, pour around 1/4 cup of batter. Cook for 2 minutes then flip the pancakes until you see defined edges.
  7. Cook for one more minute on the othr side.
  8. Enjoy!
