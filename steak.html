Steak Recipe

Description: A Steak is typically a slice of red meat, cut perpendicular to the muscle fibers, that is cooked quickly over high heat.

Ingredients

  • Steak (That's seriously all you need)

Steps

  1. Heat a heavy pan over meduium-high heat until VERY hot.
  2. Add oil to the pan.
  3. Carefully set the steak in the pan. (Use a pan that is large enough that it's not a tight fit for the steak, this could cause the pan to cool down and that will make your food steam instead of sear.)
  4. Let the steak sit for around 3-4 minutes when it releases eaisly and the bottom is a deep brown color.
  5. Continue to cook the steak for another 3-4 minutes on that bottom side.
  6. During the last minute of cooking, add 1 tbsp of butter and a few sprigs of fresh thyme to the pan witht the steaks. (Optional)
  7. Enjoy!
