Taco Recipe

Description: A Taco is a Mexican dish consisting of a fried tortilla, typically folded, filled with various mixtures, such as seasoned meat, beans, lettuce, and tomatoes.

Ingredients

  • Seasoning of Your Choice (Chili Powder, Cumin, Garlic Powder, Mexican Oregano, Salt and Pepper)
  • Toppings (Lettuce, Tomatoes, Shredded Cheese, Onions, Guacamole, Olives, Sour Cream)
  • You can Chose Between Tortillas or Hard Shells

Steps

  1. Place the ground beef in a skillet with olive oil.
  2. Brown the beef with olive oil and then drain any fat.
  3. Add the spices you chose along with tomato paste and water (or just tomato sauce)
  4. Bring the mixture to a simmer and then cook for a few more minutes unti the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the ground beef on top of a tortilla or hard shell and add your toppings of your choice.
  6. Enjoy!
