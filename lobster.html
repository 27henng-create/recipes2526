Lobster Mac & Cheese Recipe

Description: Lobster Mac & Cheese combines creamy macaroni and cheese with chunks of sweet lobster meat. It contains a rich cheesy sauce and is usaully finished with a buttery breadcrumb topping before being baked, adding more flavor and detail to the dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 Lobster meat
  • 8 ounces Cheddar Cheese
  • 1 Pound Macaroni Pasta
  • 1 1/2 Cups White Bread Crumbs
  • 1 Stick of Melted Butter
  • Roasted Garlic
  • 1/2 cup Flour
  • Water
  • 1 quart Milk

Steps

  1. Pre-heat Oven to 375.
  2. Add and cook pasta according to the package.
  3. Drain pasta.
  4. Heat the milk in a small saucepan (don't boil).
  5. In a large pot melt 6 tbsp and add the flour.
  6. Cook over low heat for around 2-3 minutes, stirring with a whisk.
  7. While whisking, add the milk and cook for 1-2 minutes more, until it is thickened and smooth.
  8. Turn off the heat and add your choice of cheese.
  9. Add the cooked macarnoi and the cooked lobster meat and stir well.
  10. Enjoy!
