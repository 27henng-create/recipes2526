Calzone Recipe

Description: A Calzone is a folded, turnover-style Italian dish made from pizza dough filled with cheese and many other ingredients, then baked or fried.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Cheese of Your Choice (Mozzarella, Parmesan, Cheddar, Feta Cheese)
  • Meat of Your Choice (Salami, Pepperoni, Canadian Bcaon, Ham, Bacon, Chicken, Italian Sausage)
  • Veggies of Your Choice (Mushrooms, Bell Peppers, Spinach, Onions)
  • Sauce (Red Pizza Sauce or White Sauce)

Steps

  1. Divide pre-made pizza dough into equal portions and roll into a circle.
  2. On one half of the dough, spread all your selected ingredients.
  3. Fold over and tightly crimp the edges.
  4. Cut air vents, bruch with oil and sprinkle on parmesan.
  5. Bake at 475F for about 15-20 minutes until golden brown. (This depends on how big the calzones will be)
  6. Enjoy!
